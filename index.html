    • Teleport: a pawn jumps across the board and takes one of your pieces.

    Against a bot you play white. Pawns promote to a queen, rook, bishop or knight. En passant, castling and draws are in. Only the Cheat Bot cheats; every other bot plays fair.

    Paste a PGN or a list of moves in standard notation, for example 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6. It must start from the normal starting position. If the PGN has WhiteElo and BlackElo, those ratings are used for the review.
